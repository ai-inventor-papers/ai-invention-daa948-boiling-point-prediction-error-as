## Full Summary

This artifact implements a complete empirical validation of the ISA (International Standard Atmosphere) barometric formula combined with the Antoine equation for water boiling point prediction. The experiment validates against two independent empirical datasets: EngineeringToolbox (125 data points, altitudes -305m to 9144m) and Deleze (130 data points, altitudes -500m to 12000m). Key findings: (1) The ISA+Antoine model achieves RMSE of 0.66°C on EngineeringToolbox data and 0.89°C on Deleze data, significantly outperforming an isothermal baseline (0.84°C and 3.00°C respectively). (2) The standard ISA lapse rate (L=0.0065 K/m) is near-optimal for EngineeringToolbox data (optimal L=0.0050 K/m, RMSE=0.40°C) but Deleze data suggests a higher lapse rate (optimal L=0.0078 K/m, RMSE=0.69°C). (3) Reference point tests at 0, 1000, 2000, 3000, 5000m show errors of 0.00, 0.02, 0.14, 0.37, 1.07°C. (4) Monotonic decrease of boiling point with altitude is verified. All headline numbers independently re-derived and verified through a separate code path.
