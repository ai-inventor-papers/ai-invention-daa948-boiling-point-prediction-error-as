## Full Summary

Compiled and validated empirical water boiling point vs altitude dataset from 5 authoritative sources (129 total examples) for ISA/Antoine model evaluation. Sources: Forbes (1857) Alps field measurements (17), Law Supply engineering tables (24), UC Davis/Snyder 2016 calibration tables (21), Engineering ToolBox industry reference (49), and SimuPipe ISA theoretical baseline (18). All data standardized to SI units (altitude in meters, pressure in hPa, boiling point in °C) with source citations. Validated against ISA barometric formula and Antoine equation - empirical mean absolute error 0.15°C, max 2.5°C (Forbes outliers). Output in exp_sel_data_out.json schema with full/mini/preview variants. Includes pyproject.toml with pinned dependencies and reproducibility.md.
